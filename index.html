Celebrating Stories
with Every Frame

When I started Story by Clicker Babu, it was more than just about clicking pictures—it was about capturing love, unfiltered joy, and everything in between. Based in Raipur and travelling across India, my shared passion for storytelling has always been driven by the authentic heartbeat of weddings.

Our journey has been nothing short of magical. Inspired by cinematic light, street photojournalism, and our love for sacred Indian traditions, we've crafted a unique signature style—a harmonious blend of unposed candid moments, fine art, and timeless royal vibes. It’s not just photography for us; it’s weaving visual heirlooms that families cherish for generations.

From documenting over 500+ destination weddings to capturing quiet tears and euphoric Haldi snans, every single wedding is a privilege. We are reminded daily of the profound trust and sacred love we've been blessed to preserve.

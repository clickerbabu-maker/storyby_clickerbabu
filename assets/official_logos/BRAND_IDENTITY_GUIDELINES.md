## 🔤 Official Typography Anatomy

1. **Primary Headline Wordmark:**
   - **Typeface:** `Cormorant Garamond` (Weights: Medium 500 & SemiBold 600)
   - **Alternative / Fallback:** `Didot` / `Playfair Display` / `Georgia`
   - **Letter Tracking:** Wide spacing (`+14px` on print / `0.35em` on web)
   - **Character Case:** UPPERCASE

2. **Tagline Subtitle (`WEDDINGS & FILMS`):**
   - **Typeface:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weight: SemiBold 600)
   - **Alternative / Fallback:** `Helvetica Neue` / `Arial`
   - **Letter Tracking:** Generous tracking (`+11px` on print / `0.45em` on web)
   - **Character Case:** UPPERCASE

3. **Center Divider:**
   - Single continuous hairline (`1.5px`) with a $45^\circ$ rotated geometric gold diamond in the exact visual center.

## 🛡️ Clear-Space & Minimum Size Rules
- Maintain at least **20% padding** around the logo perimeter free from photos or heavy graphics.
- Minimum digital display width: **180px**.
- Minimum print reproduction height: **15mm**.

Terms of Commission

Official Wedding Photography & Cinematography Commission Terms • business-online.in

1. Date Reservation & Retainer Protocol

To preserve our uncompromised standard of craftsmanship, Story by Clicker Babu accepts a strictly limited number of weddings per season. Dates are confirmed solely upon receipt of the signed commission agreement and the initial booking retainer fee. Verbal inquiries do not secure studio dates.

2. Creative Style & Artistic Discretion

Clients commission our studio for our signature editorial photojournalism, cinematic tone curves, and couture color science. While client preferences are honored, Clicker Babu maintains sole creative discretion over shot composition, lighting choreography, color grading palettes, and final frame selection.

3. Post-Production & Delivery Timelines

Our bespoke multi-layer grading and sound design process follows rigorous quality standards:

  • Editorial Highlight Teasers: Dispatched within 7–14 business days post-wedding.
  • Complete High-Resolution Master Gallery: Color-graded and delivered within 45–60 days.
  • Cinematic Feature Film & Extended Cut: Mastered in 4K resolution within 60–90 days.
